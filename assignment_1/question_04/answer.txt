FaceFare is a public-transport fare collection prototype that combines a Flask/Socket.IO app, trip and fare handling, facial-recognition integration, and wallet operations. The linked wallet function validates an adjustment, performs it in a SQLite transaction, prevents a retry from charging twice when an operation ID is supplied, and records the change in a ledger.
